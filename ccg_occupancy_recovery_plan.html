1. The Situation

Ten of our 17 settings are operating below their revenue potential — some through sharp recent declines, others through chronic underperformance. Together they generated £5.65m revenue in 2026 against a combined 2025 peak of £6.06m — a £410k revenue shortfall that flows almost entirely to EBITDA loss. The 2027 base case sits just £69k below the £1.3m target. With disciplined action on two levers — immediate cost reduction at overstaffed settings and revenue recovery through occupancy growth — the group can hit £1.3m and build meaningful headroom above it.

This is not a group-wide revenue crisis. Bramley Wood, Abacus, Merrydale, Saltway and Happitots are performing well. The problem is concentrated in ten settings. Fix these ten, protect the seven, and £1.3m is well within reach in 2027.
