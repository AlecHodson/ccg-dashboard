1. The Situation

Ten of our 17 settings are operating below their revenue potential. Together they generated £5.65m in 2026 against a 2025 peak of £6.06m — a £410k revenue shortfall that flows almost entirely to EBITDA loss. The 2027 base case sits just £69k below the £1.3m target. With two levers — immediate cost reduction at overstaffed settings and occupancy growth — the group hits £1.3m and builds headroom above it.

Across all 17 settings there are 393 families in the active pipeline (Confirmed + Contacted) and a further 94 early-stage leads (New + Viewed). 37 Waiting List enquiries are flagged for AM review — these are likely term-time or funded-only demand that does not match our year-round supply model and should not be treated as revenue pipeline.

This is not a group-wide revenue crisis. Bramley Wood, Abacus, Merrydale, Saltway and Happitots are performing well. Fix the ten under-occupied settings, protect the seven performers, and £1.3m is well within reach.
