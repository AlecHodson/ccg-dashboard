Demand Analysis — Abacus vs Hazebrouck

Enquiry pipeline and revenue comparison to inform Project Donnington — September 2026  |  Alec Hodson, Group CEO  |  CONFIDENTIAL

Verdict: Invest in the Abacus catchment — Project Donnington

Abacus generates 14.2 enquiries per month — 60% more than Hazebrouck's 8.9. At September's current roll of 60 children, Abacus produces £1,575/child/month — broadly comparable to Hazebrouck's £1,648/child/month on 31 children. The fee rates are similar. The demand volumes are not. A new setting in the Abacus catchment will fill faster, with a proven enquiry pipeline already running at 14+ per month, and 9 Contacted families waiting for a space right now.

Hazebrouck's catchment generates fewer enquiries, with more volatile monthly volume, and critically its lost reason data is almost entirely blank — making it impossible to assess suppressed demand with confidence. The Abacus catchment is the right location for Project Donnington.

Project Donnington should be in the Abacus catchment. The demand volume is 60% higher, the catchment is more consistent, and 9 families are waiting right now. Hazebrouck has a slightly higher revenue per child — but that advantage is more than offset by its rent burden (£9,659/month premises vs Abacus's £4,264) and its lower absolute enquiry volume. At September fee rates, Donnington at 90 children generates £1,700,982/year — a compelling long-term revenue base.
One nuance: Hazebrouck's enquiry rate relative to its roll size (2.6 per child vs Abacus's 2.1) suggests the Hazebrouck catchment has proportionally strong latent demand. If the Hazebrouck building were larger, it might generate more absolute enquiries. But for Project Donnington — a new setting designed to capture overflow demand — the Abacus catchment's higher absolute volume makes it the right choice.
