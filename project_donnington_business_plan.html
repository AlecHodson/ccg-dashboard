Project Donnington — Business Plan

New CCG nursery in the Abacus catchment — September 2026  |  Alec Hodson, Group CEO  |  CONFIDENTIAL — BOARD USE ONLY

Executive Summary

Project Donnington is a new CCG nursery in the Abacus catchment, Newbury. The building accommodates 15 babies in the Nest (1:3 ratio) and 24 children aged 2-5 in a single mixed-age room (1:5 for 2-3s, 1:8 for 3-5s) — 39 children at full capacity. At Abacus's September 2026 fee rate of £1,575/child/month, full capacity generates £737k revenue and £338k EBITDA (£269k net EBITDA after HO overhead allocation).

The lease is accounted for under IFRS 16 / FRS 102 (revised). The £70,000 annual lease charge is capitalised — it does not appear in EBITDA. Instead, a Right-of-Use (ROU) asset of £518,733 sits on the balance sheet, depreciated at £51,873/year. Interest on the lease liability starts at £33,393 in Year 1 and reduces annually as the liability amortises. This treatment improves reported EBITDA by £70,000 vs the old operating lease approach, but total P&L cost in Year 1 (depreciation + interest = £85,266) exceeds the old £70,000 charge by £15,266 — reducing in later years as interest falls.

Contribution breakeven is reached at Month 6 (24 children). EBIT breakeven requires additional children above that level due to the depreciation charge. The Nest is the strategic engine — keeping it full protects against the school leaver cohort cycle.

The School Leaver Cycle

An August baby turns 4 in August and starts school the following September — the youngest in their year. A September baby turns 4 in September but waits until the next September — leaving aged nearly 5. This 12-month spread clusters children into cohorts, creating bumper leaver years roughly every 4 years.

leavers. Nest covers most. Small external top-up needed.

14-16
leavers. Nest provides 7-8. Gap of 6-8 from external waiting list.
Mitigation
Full Nest
Always keep Nest at 15. Flag bumper year 12 months ahead from Room 2 age profile.
The Nest is revenue protection, not just a baby room. A full Nest of 15 guarantees ~7-8 internal transitions per year. In a bumper leaver year this covers roughly half the gap — the rest comes from the waiting list. Never deprioritise Nest marketing just because Room 2 is full.

IFRS 16 reduces the cash working capital requirement.
