Executive Summary

Project Donnington is a new CCG nursery in the Abacus catchment, Newbury. The building accommodates 15 babies in the Nest (1:3 ratio) and 24 children aged 2-5 in a single mixed-age room (1:5 for 2-3s, 1:8 for 3-5s) — 39 children at full capacity. Using Abacus's actual September 2026 fee rate of £1,575/child/month, the setting generates £737k revenue at full capacity and £213k net EBITDA after HO overhead.

Contribution breakeven is reached at 22-24 children — achievable by Month 6 given the 9 Abacus pipeline families and 14.2 enquiries/month in the catchment. The setting opens with 6 staff and scales to a steady-state team of 13 staff at full capacity.

The Nest is the strategic engine of the setting. Babies who join the Nest at 3-12 months transition into the 2-5 room at age 2, providing a protected internal pipeline that insulates the setting from the annual school leaver cycle. Keeping the Nest full is the single most important operational priority.

The School Leaver Cycle — Why the Nest Matters

Children leave for school each September. The timing of their birthday determines when they go: an August baby turns 4 in August and starts school the following September aged 4 years 0 months — the youngest in their year. A September baby turns 4 in September but must wait until the next September to start school — meaning they leave aged 4 years 11 months, nearly a full year older. This 12-month spread causes children to cluster into cohorts, and every 3-4 years a particularly large group exits together.

Typical year
8-10
school leavers in September. Nest provides 7-8 transitions. Small external top-up needed.
Leaner year
5-7
school leavers. Nest transitions exceed leavers. Room 2 may briefly overfill — manage waitlist.
Bumper year (~every 4 yrs)
14-16
school leavers. Nest provides only 7-8. Gap of 6-8 must come from external enquiries.
Mitigation
Full Nest
Always keep the Nest at or near 15. This maximises internal pipeline and minimises external dependency.
The Nest is not just a baby room — it is a revenue protection mechanism. A full Nest of 15 babies guarantees ~7-8 internal transitions per year into Room 2. In a bumper leaver year, this covers roughly half the gap. The other half must come from the external enquiry pipeline — which is why maintaining the Abacus catchment's 14+ enquiries/month is critical even at full capacity. Never deprioritise Nest marketing just because Room 2 is full.

Bumper leaver year management (Year 4 and every ~4 years thereafter): When a large September cohort exits Room 2, revenue temporarily dips. The mitigation is threefold: (1) Keep the Nest at 15 — this guarantees 7-8 internal transitions; (2) Maintain an active external waiting list for the 2-5 room so vacancies fill within weeks, not months; (3) Flag the expected bumper year 12 months in advance using the current Room 2 age profile — if you can see a large September-born cohort in Room 2, start building the waiting list now.
