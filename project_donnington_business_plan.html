Project Donnington — Business Plan

New nursery setting in the Abacus catchment — September 2026  |  Alec Hodson, Group CEO  |  CONFIDENTIAL — BOARD USE ONLY

Executive Summary

Project Donnington is a new CCG nursery setting to be located within 1 mile of Abacus Day Nursery in Newbury. Abacus is full with 60 children on roll and generates 14.2 enquiries per month — demand it cannot serve. Donnington is designed to capture this overflow, starting with the 9 families currently in the Abacus pipeline and growing through the Abacus catchment's proven enquiry volume.

Using Abacus's actual September 2026 revenue per child of £1,575/month, the setting reaches contribution breakeven at 20 children (Stage 2). At full capacity of 90 children, Donnington generates £1.04m contribution and £830k net EBITDA after HO overhead — making it one of the group's highest-contributing settings.

The staffing model is built from the ground up using Ofsted ratio requirements, CCG qualification rules, and Abacus's own staff structure as the benchmark. The model opens lean (6 staff) and scales to 19 staff at capacity — with every growth stage defined by specific children numbers, room configuration and qualification requirements.

Stage 1 loss is expected and manageable. Opening with 9 children generates a £110k annual loss — approximately £9,150/month. This is the investment cost of opening. The key is reaching Stage 2 (20 children) as quickly as possible — the 5 imminent Abacus pipeline families plus ongoing enquiry conversion should achieve this within 3-6 months of opening.
Stage 2 breakeven (20 children) is achievable within 3-6 months. The 5 families currently booking visits at Abacus are the core opening cohort. At 14.2 enquiries/month in the Abacus catchment and a 25% conversion rate, Donnington adds approximately 3-4 new children per month from new enquiries. Combined with the pipeline families, 20 children within 6 months of opening is realistic.
