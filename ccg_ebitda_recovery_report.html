1. Executive Summary

The group delivered £1.65m EBITDA in 2025, comfortably above the £1.3m rolling target. However, 2026 has seen a material deterioration driven by revenue decline across multiple settings — particularly Northumberland, Saltway, Smart Tots and Our Den — combined with a cost base that has not contracted proportionally. The 2026 estimated EBITDA of £803k represents a £497k shortfall against the £1.3m target.

The 2027 forecast, based on Famly revenue data for Jan–Jun 2027, projects a full-year EBITDA of £1.23m — within £69k of target. Closing that gap requires: (1) urgent cost action at four critical settings, (2) conversion of the 523 live Famly enquiries into enrolled children, and (3) protecting the strong margin performance at Bramley Wood, Abacus and Merrydale.

The path to £1.3m is achievable in 2027 — but it requires decisive action in Q4 2026 on staffing costs at Finchampstead, Lower Earley, Cedars and Northumberland, and a group-wide focus on converting the existing enquiry pipeline before the January 2027 intake.

Premises Costs

Premises costs (rent, rates, utilities, cleaning) are largely fixed and represent 5–15% of revenue at most settings. The outliers are Bramley Wood (£142k) and Hazebrouck (£122k) — both driven by lease commitments. These are not discretionary but should be factored into any lease renewal negotiations.

Key Cost Reduction Opportunities

  • Finchampstead: Staffing costs of £228k on £165k revenue — immediate restructure required. Target: reduce to £110k personnel (ratio-compliant at 50% of target £220k revenue).
  • Lower Earley: £323k personnel on £379k revenue. Target: reduce to £250k or grow revenue to £450k+.
  • Northumberland: £139k personnel on £175k revenue. Revenue recovery in 2027 (£211k H1) will self-correct if hiring is frozen until occupancy recovers.
  • Smart Tots: £251k personnel on £321k revenue. 48 live enquiries in Famly — revenue recovery is the primary lever here, not cost cuts.
  • Cedars: £296k personnel on £357k revenue. Explore shared management model with Boot Farm.
