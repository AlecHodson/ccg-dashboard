1. Executive Summary

The group delivered £1.65m EBITDA in 2025, above the £1.3m rolling target. With corrected full-year 2026 revenue of £11.21m (broadly flat vs £11.37m in 2025), the 2026 EBITDA shortfall is more moderate than initially shown — an estimated £859k, representing a £441k gap to target. The primary drivers are elevated salary:revenue ratios at four settings (Finchampstead, Lower Earley, Northumberland, Cedars) and a revenue mix shift, not a group-wide revenue collapse.

The 2027 forecast projects £1.23m — within £69k of target. Three levers close the gap: (1) cost action at critical settings, (2) conversion of 523 live Famly enquiries, and (3) protecting margin at Bramley Wood, Abacus and Merrydale while Happitots and Saltway sustain their strong positions.

The path to £1.3m in 2027 is very achievable — the group is operating at near-2025 revenue levels. The issue is cost structure at a handful of settings, not a fundamental revenue problem.
