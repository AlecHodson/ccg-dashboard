1. Executive Summary

The group delivered £1.65m EBITDA in 2025, above the £1.3m rolling target. 2026 has seen material deterioration driven by revenue decline across multiple settings — particularly Northumberland (−46% YoY), Saltway, Smart Tots and Our Den — with a cost base that has not contracted proportionally. The 2026 estimated EBITDA of £803k represents a £497k shortfall.

The 2027 forecast projects £1.23m — within £69k of target. Three levers close the gap: (1) cost action at four critical settings, (2) conversion of 523 live Famly enquiries, and (3) protecting margin at Bramley Wood, Abacus and Merrydale while Happitots and Saltway complete their recovery.

The path to £1.3m in 2027 is achievable — but requires decisive staffing action at Finchampstead, Lower Earley, Cedars and Northumberland in Q4 2026, and a group-wide focus on converting the existing enquiry pipeline before the January 2027 intake.
