Correction from previous analysis: Hazebrouck has 48 children on roll, not 15. The Famly enrolled count only captures children who enquired and registered since January 2026 — it misses children already on roll before that date. This correction materially improves the Scenario A numbers. Hazebrouck's actual revenue per child is £14,823/year (£1,235/month), reflecting longer hours and higher fees than the group average.

Recommendation: Scenario A — Strongly Recommended

With 48 children on roll, Scenario A is overwhelmingly the right decision. Closing Hazebrouck and moving the 48 children and 10 staff directly into a new Abacus-area setting generates £340,159 contribution from Day 1 — before a single Abacus overflow family joins. After accounting for the Hazebrouck premises saving and lost Hazebrouck contribution, the group is +£231,496 better off vs the status quo immediately.

This is not a medium-term recovery play — it is an immediate EBITDA improvement. The 48 children bring their full revenue with them. The 10 staff bring their costs with them. The only new cost is the lease (£70,000/year). The net result is that the group swaps a declining, rent-paying Hazebrouck for a growing, strategically located new setting — with positive EBITDA from the first day of trading.

Scenario B (keep Hazebrouck, buy freehold, recruit new team) remains deeply negative until Year 2 and generates far less upside. Scenario C (do nothing) continues the 13%/year Hazebrouck revenue decline whilst leaving 91 Abacus families per year unserved.

5. Operational Steps — Scenario A

  1. Property search (Month 1–3): Commission commercial agent — leasehold space within 1 mile of Abacus, sufficient for 48+ children. Ofsted registration capacity minimum 60 children (to allow growth). Target lease below £80k/year.
  2. Decision point (Month 3): Once lease terms agreed, confirm Scenario A with the board. Set a Hazebrouck closure date 3 months out.
  3. Notify Hazebrouck families (Month 3–4): Statutory minimum notice. Offer all 48 enrolled children guaranteed places at the new setting. Frame as an upgrade — new building, same team, same fees. Retention of all 48 children is the priority.
  4. Staff transfer (Month 4–5): All 10 Hazebrouck staff transfer to the new setting. No redundancies. New setting opens with an experienced, Ofsted-familiar team.
  5. Abacus overflow campaign (Month 5 onwards): Contact all 91 families who enquired at Abacus in 2026 and were turned away. Offer places at the new setting. Also convert the 16 active Hazebrouck pipeline enquiries.
  6. Hazebrouck closes (Month 6): Serve lease notice. Check dilapidations liability. Closure should coincide with new setting opening to avoid any revenue gap.
Key risk to manage: Retaining all 48 Hazebrouck children through the move. Some families may use the change as an opportunity to look elsewhere. The retention rate assumption in this model is 100% — a 10% attrition rate would reduce Day 1 contribution by approximately £69,016, which is still a positive net position vs the status quo. Communicate early, communicate clearly, and make the move feel seamless.
