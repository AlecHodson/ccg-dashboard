CCG Strategic Options Analysis

Hazebrouck & Abacus — Three-Scenario Assessment — September 2026  |  Alec Hodson, Group CEO  |  CONFIDENTIAL — BOARD USE ONLY

Recommendation: Scenario A — Close Hazebrouck, Transfer to Stepping Stones, Lease Near Abacus

Scenario A is the strongest option. Closing Hazebrouck and transferring the 10 staff and 15 children to Stepping Stones eliminates the £117,757/year premises cost, and deploys the existing team directly into the new Abacus-area lease — with zero recruitment cost. The new setting reaches near-breakeven by Year 3 and turns positive vs the status quo in Year 4.

Scenario B (keep Hazebrouck, buy freehold, recruit new team for Abacus) costs £358,453/year more than Scenario A and does not recover to positive vs the status quo within the 4-year modelling window. It is viable but significantly more expensive.

Scenario C (do nothing) avoids capital risk but leaves Abacus demand unmet and Hazebrouck declining at approximately 13%/year. Inaction is not neutral — it is a slow erosion of a £226,420 contribution that will compound over time.

5. The Abacus Demand Case

Abacus generated 127 enquiries in 2026 but enrolled only 20 children. 91 families were turned away — purely due to capacity. At 14.1 enquiries/month, a nearby setting has an immediate, proven demand pool. This is not a greenfield market entry — it is serving an existing, demonstrated demand that the group is currently unable to meet.

Year 1 ramp risk is low. The new setting starts with 16 warm Hazebrouck pipeline enquiries and access to the 91 Abacus families turned away in 2026. This is achievable without any new marketing spend.

6. Operational Steps — Scenario A

  1. Property search (Month 1–3): Commission commercial agent to identify leasehold space within 1 mile of Abacus. Target lease below £80k/year. Prioritise quick-access properties that can open within 3 months of lease signing.
  2. Decision point (Month 3): Once lease terms are agreed, confirm Scenario A with the board and set a Hazebrouck closure date.
  3. Notify Hazebrouck families (Month 3–4): Statutory minimum notice. Offer all 15 enrolled children places at Stepping Stones or the new Abacus-area setting. Proactively contact the 16 active pipeline enquiries and offer them places at the new setting.
  4. Staff transfer (Month 4–5): 2 staff to Stepping Stones (to cover transferred children); 8 staff to new Abacus-area setting. No redundancies, no recruitment.
  5. New setting opens (Month 5–6): Experienced team, immediate pipeline of warm leads, Abacus brand reputation in the same catchment.
  6. Hazebrouck closes (Month 6): Serve lease notice. Check dilapidations liability — factor into closure cost. Aim for closure to coincide with or follow new setting opening to avoid a revenue gap.
Timeline: Property search (1–3 months) → Lease signed → Notice served → Staff transfer and fit-out (2–3 months) → New setting opens → Hazebrouck closes. Total elapsed: 4–6 months from decision to new setting open.
